KEFARM is dedicated to empowering farmers in Kenya by leveraging modern technology to enhance agricultural productivity, streamline food distribution, and ensure food security. Crop Variety Recommendations by Region

Select the best crop variety for your region to maximize yield and quality.

Maize

  • Eldoret, Nakuru: H6218 (High yield, cold-resistant)
  • Kisumu, Kericho: WH505 (Medium altitude, disease-resistant)
  • Narok, Bomet: SC Duma 43 (Low rainfall, drought-tolerant)
  • Nyeri, Kakamega: P 30G19 (Well-balanced growth)
  • Mombasa: SC Sungura 301 (Coastal conditions, heat-tolerant)

Beans

  • Eldoret, Nakuru: Rosecoco (High yield, well-draining soils)
  • Kisumu, Kericho: Mwitemania (Resistant to diseases, early maturing)
  • Narok, Bomet: Yellow Beans (Drought-resistant, high protein content)
  • Nyeri, Kakamega: Nyota Beans (Fast maturing, good market value)
  • Mombasa: Wairimu Beans (Adapted to coastal climate)

Wheat

  • Eldoret, Nakuru: Robin (High altitude, high yield)
  • Kisumu, Kericho: Eagle 10 (Medium altitude, disease-resistant)
  • Narok, Bomet: Kwale (Drought-resistant, good flour quality)
  • Nyeri, Kakamega: Kenya Kasuku (Adapted for medium rainfall)
  • Mombasa: Kenya Wren (Heat-resistant, coastal adaptation)

Rice

  • Eldoret, Nakuru: Basmati 217 (Aromatic, long grain)
  • Kisumu, Kericho: Sindano (Irrigated rice, high yield)
  • Narok, Bomet: IR2793-80-1 (Drought-resistant variety)
  • Nyeri, Kakamega: Komboka (Fast-maturing, high yielding)
  • Mombasa: Pishori (Best suited for coastal climate)
